# T13.3.13 — Staff Portal Integrity

**Release identity:** `r4.10.6-t13.3.13-staff-portal-integrity`  
**Application version:** `4.10.6`

## Purpose

This source increment keeps the small-business portal architecture and closes narrowly bounded staff identity, SIFR, checklist, clocking and rendering defects. It does not redesign the portal, replace authentication, alter role definitions, or introduce a new state-management framework.

## Corrections

- StaffPortal is remounted when the authenticated identity or MFA ceremony changes, so one employee's MFA, document, incident or training drafts cannot survive into another employee session.
- The one-second operational clock is isolated from the portal controller and is displayed explicitly in the store timezone.
- SIFR reporter, store, business date, timestamp, lifecycle status and initial replies are server-owned.
- SIFR creation, management replies and status changes use narrow server RPCs; replies are row-locked and server-attributed.
- Store managers cannot use generic whole-row SIFR updates.
- Sensitive-report wording now states honestly that authorised management can see the verified identity.
- Checklist submission requires every configured item to be complete, preventing partial or rapid duplicate audit records.
- First-ever clock actions are serialised even before an employee app-state row exists.
- Ref-backed single-flight guards protect authentication, clock, checklist, cover, document and SIFR actions from same-tick double submission.
- Secure documents open from the original click gesture, avoiding delayed-popup failures on mobile browsers.
- Seven dead StaffPortal inputs were removed without changing behaviour.

## Structural StaffPortal boundary

Within the same T13.3.13 source identity, the React portal was subsequently decomposed into bounded Auth, Dashboard, Checklist, Academy, Documents, SIFR and Knowledge Base owners. The root controller is now a small shell; the complete module is intentionally not described as 90% smaller. Staff panels retain the original active-route lifetime: unfinished local drafts reset on staff-tab changes, inactive panels do not remain mounted, and the auth-scope key still clears every local state on employee or MFA-ceremony replacement.
